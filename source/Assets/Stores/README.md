# Store branding assets

- `steam-logo-lockup-white.png` is the white Steam wordmark with the circular badge and transparent Steam symbol, on a transparent background.
- `steam-badge-white-cutout.png` is the badge only. The disk is white, the Steam symbol is transparent, and the area outside the badge is transparent.

The wordmark is based on the [Valve Developer Community Steam logo](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/File:Logo-Steam-white.png). The badge is based on [Steam icon logo.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Steam_icon_logo.svg). Steam and its logo are trademarks of Valve Corporation.
